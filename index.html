How to Calculate Your Typing Speed (WPM)

Our completely free Words Per Minute (WPM) tool tracks your typing mechanics globally in real-time. Standard WPM is calculated by taking the total number of characters typed, dividing that figure by 5 (the accepted standard word length), and then normalizing it against the elapsed time duration.

Tips for Improving Accuracy and Form

  • Home Row Positioning: Always return your fingers to the home row positions (ASDF and JKL;).
  • Maintain Rhythm: Focus on smooth, steady keystrokes rather than fast bursts. Accuracy naturally leads to higher baseline speeds.
  • Ergonomics: Keep your wrists straight, sit up straight, and look straight into the screen text display field instead of downwards toward individual keys.
